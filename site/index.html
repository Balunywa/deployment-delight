Where it runs

Two ways to run it.
Choose per customer.

Most of your customers don't use Azure — they just want your software. Some need it inside their own Azure for data, security or contract reasons. Cloud Delivery handles both with the same product setup.

Most common

Hosted in your Azure

Each customer gets their own dedicated environment in your Azure. You run it and keep it up to date. The customer's IT team doesn't need to do anything — their users just sign in.

  • A separate subscription per customer — their data never mixes with anyone else's
  • No Azure account, install or access needed from the customer
  • Users sign in with their own work accounts
  • You see and update every customer from one place

When the customer requires it

In the customer's own Azure

Installed inside the customer's Azure. Their Azure admin reviews what will be installed and approves limited access with a link — no passwords shared.

Already on Azure

Plugs into their existing network, DNS, security tools and rules — without changing them.

New to Azure

A secure Azure foundation is set up first, with their permission, then your product on top.

Either way: the same product setup, the same deployment process, the same updates.

When it runs in the customer's Azure

Easy for your customer's IT team to say yes.

Your customer's Azure admin gets a link with your name on it. They see exactly what will be installed, confirm a few settings we found for them, and approve limited access they can take back anytime. No passwords or keys are shared.

  • They see what will be installed before giving access
  • Access limited to one area of their Azure
  • Their own rules are checked before every deployment
  • They can remove access at any time
