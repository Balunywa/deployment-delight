Your customer's side

Easy for your customer's IT team to say yes.

Your customer's Azure admin gets a link with your name on it. They see exactly what will be installed, confirm a few settings we found for them, and approve limited access they can take back anytime. No passwords or keys are shared.

  • They see what will be installed before giving access
  • Access limited to one area of their Azure
  • Their own rules are checked before every deployment
  • They can remove access at any time
